Gabi Alves

Tech Lead and data engineer in Curitiba, working at the intersection of data acquisition, software architecture, and technical leadership.

I design systems that turn official publications and complex documents into reliable, traceable data.

At Legalbot, I lead Data Engineering across architecture, code standards, reviews, mentoring, and the modernization of legacy capture pipelines.

I started closer to the code, specializing in crawlers, spiders, and complex PDF parsers for regulatory collection. Before that, I built judicial-data robots, internal documentation, and observability.

I care about systems that last: data quality, useful documentation, maintainable legacy code, and processes that help teams reason together. I’m finishing a specialization in Agile Software Development while studying Big Data and AI.

My interests move between systems thinking, software architecture, engineering management, neuroscience, and the books that change how I understand all of them. This site is where those threads meet.
